# Account Security Policy

## High-Risk Case Definition
An account security case is high risk when a customer reports:
- unauthorized login
- suspicious password reset
- account takeover
- unknown account changes
- suspicious billing combined with account access concerns

## Support Agent Responsibilities
Support agents should:
- acknowledge the concern
- avoid making claims about completed account actions unless confirmed by the system
- route the case for secure review when needed
- recommend the customer follow official security steps if defined by policy

## Safe Support Language
Allowed examples:
- "We take account security concerns seriously."
- "Your case will be reviewed."
- "We recommend updating your password if you still have account access."

## Unsafe Support Language
Support agents must not say:
- "We already secured your account."
- "Your account has been restored."
- "We verified your identity."
- "We reset your password."
- "We removed the attacker from your account."

## Human Review Rule
Human review is required for:
- account takeover claims
- identity-sensitive changes
- password reset confirmation
- email or phone changes related to account recovery
