# Shipping FAQ

## Standard Shipping Time
Standard shipping usually takes **3 to 5 business days** after the order has been processed.

## Express Shipping Time
Express shipping usually takes **1 to 2 business days** after processing.

## Order Processing Time
Most orders are processed within **24 hours** on business days.
Orders placed on weekends or holidays may be processed on the next business day.

## Delayed Shipments
Shipping delays may happen because of:
- carrier issues
- weather conditions
- customs checks
- high seasonal demand

## Customer Communication Guidance
Support agents should:
- provide estimated shipping timelines
- avoid guaranteeing exact delivery dates unless tracking data confirms them
- encourage customers to check tracking information if available

## What Support Agents Must Not Promise
Support agents must not:
- guarantee same-day shipping unless explicitly confirmed in the order system
- promise compensation for delays unless approved by policy or human review
- invent delivery dates
