# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
Refund requests may be reviewed in cases such as:
- duplicate charge
- failed order fulfillment
- charged but order not delivered
- unauthorized billing issue
- subscription billing error

## Refund Approval Rule
Support agents **must not approve or guarantee refunds directly** unless a policy-approved automated rule explicitly allows it.
Refund decisions that affect billing outcome require either:
- system-confirmed eligibility, or
- human review / billing team approval

## Duplicate Charge Handling
If a customer reports a duplicate charge:
1. confirm that the order and payment details are available
2. retrieve the billing or order context
3. draft a cautious response
4. route for human review when billing action or refund decision is required

## Safe Support Language
Allowed examples:
- "We will review your billing case."
- "Your request will be reviewed by the billing team."
- "We are looking into the charge you reported."

## Unsafe Support Language
Support agents must not say:
- "Your refund is confirmed."
- "We will refund you immediately."
- "Your refund has already been approved."
- "You will definitely receive a refund."

## Human Review Rule
Human approval is required for:
- refund commitment
- billing reversal
- exception handling
- unclear or disputed payment cases
