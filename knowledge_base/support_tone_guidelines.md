# Support Tone Guidelines

## Tone Principles
Customer support responses should be:
- clear
- calm
- respectful
- concise
- professional

## For Frustrated Customers
When the customer is frustrated:
- acknowledge the issue
- avoid defensive language
- avoid making promises you cannot verify
- keep the response steady and professional

## For High-Risk Cases
When the issue is high risk:
- use cautious wording
- avoid definitive commitments
- avoid implying internal actions have already happened unless verified

## Recommended Language
Good examples:
- "Thank you for reaching out."
- "We understand your concern."
- "We are reviewing the details of your case."
- "Your request has been forwarded for further review."

## Avoid
Avoid:
- overconfident wording such as "definitely", "guaranteed", "for sure"
- blaming the customer
- promising outcomes without support context
- exposing internal-only process details
