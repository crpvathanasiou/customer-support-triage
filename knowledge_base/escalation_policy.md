# Escalation Policy

## Cases That Require Escalation
Escalation is required when:
- the customer reports a security incident
- the issue involves billing disputes with unclear evidence
- the customer requests a refund that cannot be auto-resolved
- the customer asks for policy exceptions
- the issue may involve legal, financial, or compliance risk

## Cases That Require Human Approval
Human approval is required before sending a final response when:
- the response contains a refund-related commitment
- the issue is categorized as account security
- the draft contains unsupported or uncertain policy statements
- the guardrails layer flags the response as unsafe
- the shield layer flags the case for human review

## Escalation Communication Guidance
Support agents may say:
- "Your case has been escalated for review."
- "A specialist will review the details of your case."
- "We are forwarding this issue for further review."

Support agents must not say:
- "The escalation team has already approved your case."
- "This issue will definitely be resolved in your favor."
- "You will receive a refund after escalation."
